(57) Abstract

A drafter who trained as an engineer, became a filmmaker on tour with a rock band, directed campaigns for television’s biggest nights, and then asked a question nobody had settled: what does it mean to truly own something after you send it? The answer is patented.

FIG. 1  The inventionSheet 2 of 5

A file that answers only to you.

Send a file today and every copy belongs to whoever holds it. A superfile works differently. It stays encrypted on their device and asks permission every time it opens, so you can take access back at any moment.
